# Architectural & Editorial Decision Records (TruthLens)

## Decision Point 1 (DP1): Feed Ordering & Triage Prioritization
TruthLens implements an algorithmic virality-weighted triage model that computes an composite spread velocity score combining cross-platform multiplier indices (with elevated weight for dark social WhatsApp forwards), uppercase ratio, and sensational keyword triggers. This prioritization architecture guarantees that high-velocity, high-panic viral assertions surface immediately at the apex of investigator queues before irreversible information cascades achieve community saturation. Simultaneously, newsroom investigators and public citizens retain instant one-click access to chronologically ordered streams and thematic desks, ensuring total transparent visibility without sacrificing rapid-response containment capability.

## Decision Point 2 (DP2): Visibility of Unverified Claims in Public Feeds
Unverified submissions remain visible in the public intake feed and investigative dossier archive, but are strictly quarantined with prominent `[UNVERIFIED / TRIAGE]` stamp seals and high-visibility forensic hazard warnings. Hiding or suppressing unverified claims entirely creates a dangerous informational vacuum in which rumors continue circulating unchecked across closed peer-to-peer networks without public indication that a rigorous journalistic inquiry is underway. By displaying these nascent claims accompanied by real-time deepfake probability meters and heuristic risk flags (`[SENSATIONAL]`, `[HIGH_CAPS]`, `[UNSOURCED]`), TruthLens inoculates citizens against uncritical forwarding while actively signaling that institutional verification is in progress.

## Decision Point 3 (DP3): Post-Submission Editing & Audit Policy
Original ingested viral text, source URLs, attached media banners, and intake timestamps are rendered permanently immutable upon receipt to maintain strict forensic chain-of-custody. Editorial investigators and submitting tipsters are prohibited from modifying or retroactively altering raw viral claims; instead, all supplementary context, peer review votes, and synthesized fact-check summaries must be appended to an immutable, versioned audit ledger. This architectural constraint protects the evidentiary record against retroactive narrative shifting, establishing tamper-evident accountability suitable for electoral monitoring, regulatory review, and public trust.
